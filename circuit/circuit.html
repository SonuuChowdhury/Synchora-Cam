Synchora-Cam Flashing & Wiring

AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM (OV2640) + Arduino UNO (CH340) USB-to-UART Bridge with 5V to 3.3V Level Shifting

UART PIN MAP Connection Table

  • Uno 5V: ➔ ESP32-CAM 5V (VCC in)
  • Uno GND: ➔ ESP32-CAM GND
  • Uno RESET: ➔ Uno GND (holds ATmega halted)
  • Uno RX (D0): ➔ 1kΩ / 2kΩ Divider ➔ ESP32-CAM U0R
  • Uno TX (D1): ➔ ESP32-CAM U0T (Direct line)
  • ESP32 GPIO 0: ➔ GND (during flashing only)

SAFETY Level Shifter Specs

  • R1 (Series): 1kΩ (1/4W metal or carbon film)
  • R2 (Pull-down): 2kΩ (to Common GND)
  • Output Voltage: 5.0V × 2/3 = 3.33V
  • Protection: Prevents 5V UART signals from burning out ESP32 3.3V IO gates.

FLASH PROTOCOL Step-by-Step

  • 1. Wire circuit and bridge GPIO0 to GND.
  • 2. Connect USB & press ESP32-CAM RST button.
  • 3. Flash with: pio run -t upload
  • 4. Disconnect GPIO0 from GND and press RST.
  • 5. Status LED glows solid when connected!
